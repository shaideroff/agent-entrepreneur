# Knowledge and Evidence

Profiles are references, not endorsements or impersonations. Attribute ideas and methods to documented sources. Do not reproduce copyrighted books, paid courses or substantial interview transcripts.

Evidence levels: A = primary evidence plus independent corroboration; B = credible traceable documentation; C = emerging or limited public evidence; unverified = discovery candidates or founder-contributed material awaiting corroboration. A profile-level label is not blanket validation of every claim.

S Haider's Built from Zero record is founder-contributed and marked unverified. Its working stages are discover, build, sell, systemise and scale. It is not represented as independently validated research.

Store source metadata, URLs, lawful short excerpts where permitted and original summaries. Respect source terms and licences.

## Claim-level provenance and specialist evaluation

Use [the claim evidence schema](claim-evidence-schema.json) for material factual, quantitative, causal and prescriptive claims. Keep claim status, source type, publication/access dates, scope, limitations and contradictions attached to the claim rather than relying only on a person- or profile-level grade.

Use [the specialist evaluation rubrics](specialist-rubrics.md) for the six analytical disciplines. Each specialist must separate evidence, assumptions, options, risks, unknowns and measurable decision gates. Disagreements remain explicit until evidence resolves them; do not manufacture consensus.

A JSON Schema validates record shape, not truth. Source quality, whether a source actually supports the claim, applicability to the founder's context and freshness still require human or model-assisted review.
