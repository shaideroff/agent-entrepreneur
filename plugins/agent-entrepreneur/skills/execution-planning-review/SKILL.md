# Execution planning and review

## Objective

Translate an approved objective into an operating plan with clear ownership, dependencies, measurable outcomes and review gates. Also use this workflow to evaluate completed work and adapt the plan.

## Planning workflow

1. Confirm the outcome. Define the business outcome, baseline, target, deadline, geography and non-negotiable constraints.
2. Decompose work. Break the outcome into workstreams, deliverables and tasks. Avoid activity lists that do not produce verifiable outputs.
3. Assign accountability. Identify one directly responsible owner per deliverable, required collaborators and decision authority. If the user has no team, design a solo-operator plan with realistic capacity.
4. Sequence dependencies. Map prerequisites, critical path, external dependencies, approvals and long-lead items.
5. Estimate capacity. Distinguish calendar duration from effort. Include operating load, contingency, budget and cash timing where known.
6. Define metrics. Give each workstream a leading indicator, lagging outcome metric, baseline, target, cadence and data source.
7. Install decision gates. Define go/no-go, stop/continue, escalation and scope-reduction criteria.
8. Set cadence. Provide weekly execution reviews and periodic strategic checkpoints appropriate to the stage.

## Review workflow

1. Compare actuals with baseline and target over the same period and definitions.
2. Separate output delivered from outcome achieved.
3. Identify variance, root causes, external factors and data quality issues.
4. Preserve failed experiments and negative evidence; do not rewrite history to make the plan appear successful.
5. Update assumptions, priorities, forecasts and owners.
6. Record decisions, actions, due dates and the next review checkpoint.

## Required output

- Outcome and baseline/target
- Roadmap by phase and workstream
- Deliverables, owner, due date and dependencies
- Capacity/budget assumptions and risks
- KPI scorecard with formulas and data sources
- Weekly operating cadence
- Decision gates and contingency triggers
- Review log and next actions

## Guardrails

- Do not invent owners, commitments, dates, budgets or KPI baselines.
- If a solo founder is executing, keep work-in-progress low and sequence the critical path.
- Do not mark a task complete without evidence of the deliverable or outcome.
- Do not send messages, publish, spend money, create accounts or alter external systems without explicit user approval.
