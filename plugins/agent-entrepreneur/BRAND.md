# Brand identity

## Concept

The mark is a connected decision map: six outer nodes represent business context, evidence, options and execution dependencies; the central gold node represents the decision point. The connecting paths express the Plugin's job of turning fragmented business inputs into a structured, reviewable path forward.

## Geometry and palette

- Shared square 512 × 512 viewBox for the light and dark logo variants.
- Six perimeter nodes and one central decision node, connected by consistent rounded strokes.
- Deep evergreen (#123B35) anchors the light version; warm off-white (#E8F1ED) carries the dark version; muted gold (#D39A42 / #E5B65F) marks the central decision point.
- Compact icon uses the same geometry in a 128 × 128 viewBox and remains legible without text.
- SVGs are self-contained and use no external images, fonts, scripts or linked resources.
